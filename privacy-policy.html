Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and handled through this website.

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Julia Hamilton Knox respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This Privacy Policy describes the types of information that may be collected through the website and how that information may be used.

2. Information That May Be Collected

Depending on how the website is configured and used, the website may collect information that a visitor voluntarily provides, including:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Subject of an inquiry
  • Message or other information submitted through a contact form

The website may also collect limited technical information, such as browser type, device type, operating system, approximate location, referring website, pages visited, and date or time of access. This information may be collected through hosting, security, analytics, or website-performance services if those services are enabled.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information submitted through the website may be used to:

  • Respond to inquiries and consultation requests
  • Communicate regarding the information requested
  • Evaluate whether a requested consultation may be appropriate
  • Maintain website security and prevent abuse
  • Improve website performance and functionality
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

4. Contact Forms and Email

Information submitted through a contact form may be transmitted to the designated professional email address or to a service provider used to deliver form submissions.

Visitors should not submit confidential, privileged, sensitive, or time-critical information through this website. Submitting a form does not create an attorney-client relationship.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

The website may use third-party providers for hosting, website security, spam prevention, analytics, email delivery, form processing, or other technical functions. Those providers may process information as necessary to provide their services.

The website owner should review and update this policy if the website begins using additional services, cookies, analytics platforms, advertising tools, or other third-party technologies.

6. Cookies and Analytics

This website may use cookies or similar technologies where necessary for functionality, security, analytics, or performance. If analytics or non-essential cookies are enabled, the website should provide any notices or consent controls required by applicable law.

7. Information Sharing

Personal information is not intentionally sold or rented to third parties. Information may be disclosed to service providers, professional advisers, legal authorities, or other parties when reasonably necessary to operate the website, protect rights, prevent fraud or abuse, or comply with legal obligations.

8. Data Retention

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, to maintain business records, to address disputes, or to comply with legal obligations.

9. Data Security

Reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures should be used to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Privacy Requests

Visitors may contact the office to ask questions about information submitted through the website or to request correction or deletion where applicable.

Privacy inquiries may be sent to: info@juliahamiltonknox.com

11. Updates to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in website functionality, service providers, legal requirements, or information-handling practices. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

12. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Julia Hamilton Knox through the contact information provided on this website.
